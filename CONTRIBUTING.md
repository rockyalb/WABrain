# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in WABrain.

- **Issues are welcome**: bug reports, deployment problems, and feature ideas.
  Please do not paste real chat content, phone numbers, keys, or tokens into an
  issue.
- **Pull requests are not accepted** at the moment. WABrain is developed by a
  single author, who keeps the right to offer it commercially.
- **Forks are fine.** The [license](LICENSE.md) lets you fork, modify, and run
  WABrain for yourself, inside your own organization, and for non-commercial
  education and research. Offering it (or a substantially similar product built
  from it) to others as a commercial product or service is not permitted
  without a partnership.
- **Security issues:** report them privately through GitHub's "Report a
  vulnerability" button on the Security tab, not in a public issue.

For commercial use or partnerships, get in touch through
[github.com/rockyalb](https://github.com/rockyalb).
